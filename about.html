About IJABC

IJABC is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal covering activity and behavior computing.

Publication information

  • Publisher: Care XDX Center, Kyushu Institute of Technology
  • Online ISSN: 2759-2871
  • Subject areas: medicine, nursing, engineering, information science, psychology, and education
  • J-STAGE: International Journal of Activity and Behavior Computing

For the current issues and published articles, please visit the official J-STAGE journal page.
