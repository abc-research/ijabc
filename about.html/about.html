About IJABC

IJABC is being prepared as an academic journal website focused on activity and behavior computing.

  • Publisher: Care XDX Center, Kyushu Institute of Technology
  • Location: 2-6 Hibikino, Wakamatsu-ku, Kitakyushu, Japan
  • Publication frequency: twice a year, January and July
