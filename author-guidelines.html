Author Guidelines

Clear, concise guidance for preparing an IJABC submission.

Manuscript structure

  • Title, author names, affiliations, and corresponding author
  • Abstract and keywords
  • Introduction, methods, results, and discussion as appropriate
  • References, figures, tables, and acknowledgements

Formatting

Use the format and page limits specified for your submission category. Write for an interdisciplinary audience and define specialized terms at first use.

Ethics and transparency

Authors should report relevant ethics approvals, conflicts of interest, funding, data availability, and use of computational or generative tools where applicable.

Submission

See Submissions for category-specific information and the official ABC2027 website for current instructions.
