Author Guidelines

International Journal of Activity and Behavior Computing (IJABC)

This page is based on the IJABC author-guidelines document dated November 25, 2023. Please check the latest call for papers before submitting.

Submission and review

  • New submissions are considered in synchronization with the International Conference on Activity and Behavior Computing.
  • The online submission system opens four weeks before the deadline and closes at Anywhere on Earth on the deadline date.
  • IJABC uses a double-blind review process. Submissions should be anonymized.
  • Remove author names and affiliations, while retaining relevant self-citations.

Manuscript format

  • Length: 6–10 pages.
  • Use the IEEE conference template.
  • Use the abbreviated title: “Int’l J. Activity and Behavior Computing”.
  • Include a descriptive title, a 150–200 word abstract, and references in the template format.

After acceptance

Accepted papers proceed through final comments, submission of source files and PDF, editorial checks, and publication. Final materials should no longer be anonymized.

Published papers are open access on J-STAGE, assigned a DOI, and published under a CC BY 4.0 license. Copyright remains with the authors.
