Author Guidelines

These guidelines summarize the current ABC2027 CFP. Always confirm the active call and portal at the official ABC2027 website.

Regular papers

  • IJABC regular papers: 6–15 pages.
  • IEEE Proceedings papers: 4–10 pages, subject to approval.
  • Use the IEEE conference template unless the active CFP specifies another format.
  • Include a descriptive title, concise abstract, complete references, and clear technical content.

Double-blind review

  • Remove author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, and other identifying information from the review manuscript.
  • Retain relevant self-citations in a normal scholarly manner.

Other categories

  • Position papers: 2–4 pages; not intended for journal publication.
  • Challenge papers: format and publication details will be announced separately.

After acceptance

Final materials should no longer be anonymized. Published IJABC papers are open access on J-STAGE and assigned a DOI.
