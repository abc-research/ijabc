Author Guidelines

International Journal of Activity and Behavior Computing

November 25th, 2023

Dates and submission

New submissions will be considered in synchronization with the International Conference on Activity and Behavior Computing. The deadline changes each year; please see the Call for Papers.

Paper acceptance will usually be finalized within 12 weeks after the deadline. The online submission system will open 4 weeks before the deadline and close at Anywhere on Earth time on the deadline date.

Double-blind review and anonymization

Submissions must be anonymized because IJABC employs a double-blind review process. Papers that do not comply with the anonymization policy may be desk rejected.

Remove author names and affiliations from the paper. Relevant prior publications must still be cited and must not be replaced with “blank for blind review.” If overlapping work is under simultaneous submission elsewhere, cite it as “In submission to...” or “To appear in...” and provide a copy to the reviewers through the submission system or editors.

Page length

Submissions must be 6–10 pages. Regardless of length, papers are held to a uniform, high quality bar, and the length should be commensurate with the contribution.

Template

Authors should follow the IEEE conference template.

  • Use the IJABC abbreviated title: Int’l J. Activity and Behavior Computing.
  • Placeholder metadata such as volume 0 and issue 0 is acceptable during review.
  • Minor formatting or metadata concerns will be handled at the final-submission stage.
  • Color graphics are welcome, but figures should remain readable in black-and-white print.

Required manuscript elements

  • Descriptive title: Clearly and accurately state what the paper is about.
  • Abstract: 150–200 words, with objectives, results, and main conclusions; omit future plans and citations.
  • References: Follow the template and current or past IJABC issues for citation examples.
  • Manuscript history: For a paper previously rejected by IJABC, provide the reviewer comments, a summary of changes, and clearly highlight the changes in the manuscript.

After acceptance

  1. The Editors will send an acceptance notification and may include final comments for the camera-ready manuscript.
  2. IJABC will request source files in Word or LaTeX and PDFs. The final document must no longer be anonymized. The production manager will provide DOI, volume, and issue details.
  3. Editors will check publisher suitability and copyright permissions, including permissions for third-party material.
  4. The paper will be published in an issue following the notification decision. Missed post-acceptance deadlines may delay publication to the next cycle.

Open access and copyright

Published papers are open access on the J-STAGE platform, with DOI assignment and no embargo. Copyright remains with the authors, who must agree to add a CC BY 4.0 license.

Questions

For questions about the editorial review process or assignment to an issue, contact the journal administrator: abc2024@sozolab.jp.
