Author Guidelines

International Journal of Activity and Behavior Computing (IJABC)

These guidelines summarize the current ABC2027 CFP. Always confirm the active call and portal at the official ABC2027 website.

Regular papers

  • IJABC regular papers: 6–15 pages.
  • IEEE Proceedings papers: 4–10 pages, subject to approval.
  • Use the IEEE conference template unless the active CFP specifies another format.
  • Include a descriptive title, a concise abstract, complete references, and clear technical content.

Double-blind review

  • Remove author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, and other identifying information from the review manuscript.
  • Retain relevant self-citations in a normal scholarly manner; do not replace them with “anonymous” references.

Other categories

  • Position papers: 2–4 pages; intended for emerging ideas, practical challenges, and future directions, not journal publication.
  • Challenge papers: format and publication details will be announced separately.

After acceptance

Accepted authors may be asked for final source files and a publication-ready PDF. Final materials should no longer be anonymized. Published IJABC papers are open access on J-STAGE and assigned a DOI.

Questions

For journal matters, see the contact information on the official J-STAGE page: IJABC publication information.
