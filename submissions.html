Submissions

For ABC2027, check the official CFP and submission website for the active deadline and portal.

Paper categories and format

Regular paper

IJABC: 6–15 pages. Full technical-content papers intended for journal publication.

IEEE Proceedings: 4–10 pages, subject to approval.

Position paper

2–4 pages. Emerging ideas, practical challenges, and future directions. Not intended for publication.

Challenge paper

Format and publication details: TBA in the relevant challenge announcement.

Review and preparation

  • Submissions are peer reviewed.
  • Prepare an anonymized manuscript for double-blind review.
  • Use the IEEE conference template unless the current CFP specifies otherwise.
  • Read the Author Guidelines.
