Editorial Board

For the ABC2027 cycle, IJABC’s editorial structure follows the conference review community. ABC2027 committee members support the review and publication of accepted work.

ABC2027 cycle · Sydney · 19–22 January 2027
Accepted and presented ABC2027 papers may be considered for publication in IJABC, subject to the journal’s publication checks.
